This package records iteratively repaired Java-to-Python translations of Apache
Commons CLI, CSV, FileUpload and Validator, using interface skeletons and
evaluation tests from AlphaTrans (Intelligent CAT Lab).

Upstream licenses and notices are retained under metadata/upstream-licenses/.
Dataset revision: c1cabf93d41a153de207d6b098e1da7bbd79abab
Dataset repository: https://github.com/Intelligent-CAT-Lab/AlphaTrans
Apache Commons projects: https://commons.apache.org/

The repair arms are modeled on the compiler-guided and test-guided repair loops
described by CRUST-bench (https://github.com/anirudhkhatry/CRUST-bench). This is
an adaptation to a different source language, target language and evaluation
protocol, not a reproduction of their results, and the numbers here are not
comparable to theirs.

Repair used a GitHub Copilot subscription reached through a separately
authenticated, user-run instance of the unofficial ericc-ch/copilot-api proxy on
loopback. That proxy is not affiliated with or supported by GitHub. No proxy
code, credentials or raw request/response streams are included here.

Generated outputs and benchmark observations are not upstream releases and are
not endorsed by the upstream projects. Known failures remain in the repaired
code, including commons-csv, which still does not collect cleanly in either arm.
